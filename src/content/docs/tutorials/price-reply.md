## 2. Copy this prompt

Keep the practice page active. Paste this into the Dassi sidebar and send it.

```text
Read only the customer message and confirmed terms on this practice page. Draft a short, natural English reply. State the product subtotal, not a delivered total; distinguish production lead time from transit time. Ask for a postal code and packaging requirements. Mark unknown charges as unconfirmed. Do not promise discounts or an arrival date. Show the draft in the sidebar only; do not send or fill any form.
```

## 3. Review the result

Check that the product subtotal is USD 960, freight remains unconfirmed, and 12 business days refers to production.

If the page cannot be read, ask Dassi to identify missing information before supplying it. Do not ask it to guess.

## 4. Use your own page

Open a relevant business page you are authorized to process. Adapt the product, quantity or language in the prompt. Review the draft or table before proceeding. This tutorial does not configure background auto-replies or send customer messages automatically.
