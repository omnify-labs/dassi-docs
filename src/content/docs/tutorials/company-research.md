## 2. Copy this prompt

Keep the practice page active. Paste this into the Dassi sidebar and send it.

```text
Read only this practice company profile. Summarize its business, products, explicitly listed markets, public contact channel and source link in English. Separate page facts from inferences. We sell insulated bottles: suggest one potential fit to investigate, without claiming purchasing intent. Mark buyer names, budget and sourcing plans as not found when absent. Do not collect private contact details, send messages or submit forms.
```

## 3. Review the result

The UK and Ireland are stated markets. Potential fit for insulated bottles is an inference, not evidence of purchasing intent.

If the page cannot be read, ask Dassi to identify missing information before supplying it. Do not ask it to guess.

## 4. Use your own page

Open a relevant business page you are authorized to process. Adapt the product, quantity or language in the prompt. Review the draft or table before proceeding. This tutorial does not configure background auto-replies or send customer messages automatically.
