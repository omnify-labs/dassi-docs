A 40-minute talk has maybe six ideas in it. Dassi reads the transcript and gives you the six, with the timestamp to jump back to each.

## Before you start

New to Dassi? Follow the [installation guide](/getting-started/installation/) and [model setup guide](/guides/connect-ai-provider/).

Open the video on YouTube in Chrome and check that a transcript is available (**…** → **Show transcript** under the video). Open Dassi alongside it.

## 1. Outline the video

```text
Open this video's transcript and structure it into sections. For each section give the timestamp where it starts (mm:ss), a title, and 2–4 bullet points. Include one short direct quote per section.
Use only what is said in the transcript; don't add background information.
```

## 2. Pull out what to do

```text
From those notes, list any concrete recommendations or action items the speaker gives, each with its timestamp. Then give me the whole thing as Markdown I can paste into my notes.
```

## Check the result

Click two of the timestamps and confirm the video jumps to the topic named. Auto-generated transcripts misspell names and products, so check any proper nouns before you share the notes. If there is no transcript, Dassi cannot read the audio — it will tell you rather than guess.
