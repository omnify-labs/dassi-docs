"Am I a fit?" is a question you answer by reading the requirements list twice. Dassi reads it once, against your actual resume, and shows its work.

## Before you start

New to Dassi? Follow the [installation guide](/getting-started/installation/) and [model setup guide](/guides/connect-ai-provider/).

Open the job listing in Chrome. Have your resume open in another tab or added as a [file reference](/guides/file-references/). Open Dassi on the listing tab.

## 1. Score the match

```text
Compare this job listing to my resume in "[resume tab / reference name]". List every requirement in the listing and mark each: met (cite the resume line), partly met, or gap. Give an overall match as "X of Y requirements met" — no percentage.
Don't invent experience I don't have.
```

## 2. Decide what to say

```text
For the partly-met and gap items, tell me which are likely deal-breakers and which are trainable. Then rewrite my resume summary in 3 bullets to lead with the requirements I meet best, using only facts already in my resume.
```

## Check the result

Every "met" should cite a real line from your resume; check two. The deal-breaker judgement is Dassi's guess about the employer — treat it as a prompt to read the listing again, not a decision. Nothing on the job site should have changed; Dassi doesn't apply or save for you here.
