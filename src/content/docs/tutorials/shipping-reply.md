New to Dassi? Follow the [installation guide](/getting-started/installation/), open the sidebar and sign in. If prompted for credits or model configuration, follow [AI Models & Providers](/guides/connect-ai-provider/).

## 2. Copy this prompt

Keep the practice page active. Paste this into the Dassi sidebar and send it.

```text
Read only this practice order and tracking record. Draft a concise English customer reply, followed by a Chinese version. Include the date and status of the latest scan and explain that no ETA is available. Do not guarantee Friday delivery, infer a lost parcel, or invent a carrier investigation. Suggest one next step for a person to verify. Display a draft only; do not send or modify the order.
```

## 3. Review the result

Check the scan date and status. A historical scan is not a live location or a delivery guarantee.

If the page cannot be read, ask Dassi to identify missing information before supplying it. Do not ask it to guess.

## 4. Use your own page

Open a relevant business page you are authorized to process. Adapt the product, quantity or language in the prompt. Review the draft or table before proceeding. This use case does not configure background auto-replies or send customer messages automatically.
