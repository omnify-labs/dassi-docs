Pick one small task, open the relevant pages, then paste the prompt below into dassi. The first time, work on 1–3 pages and check the result yourself.

## Put product details into one table

```text
Organize the three open product pages: name, price, currency, stock status, source link.
One row per product. Mark anything the page doesn't state as "not found"; don't guess.
Keep the original prices, don't convert currencies. Don't order anything or change the pages.
```

We tested this product-catalog task in BitBrowser on three public book pages from Books to Scrape. That is a demo site, not a real store; other sites need their own check.

## A customer sent their website — find out what they do

```text
Look at this company's website and summarize: what they do, main products, the markets they explicitly say they serve, and public contact details.
Cite a source link for each item. Keep facts and inferences separate; leave anything not found blank.
Summarize only — don't send emails or submit forms.
```

## Too many reviews — find the recurring problems

```text
Organize the reviews visible on the current page, grouped by the problems people mention.
Keep the original quotes for each group and count how many reviews you actually read.
State the scope you read; don't treat a few reviews as every buyer's verdict.
```

The last two are templates to try, not something verified on your business site. If a page can't be read, have dassi say why rather than make up a result.
