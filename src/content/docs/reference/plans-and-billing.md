dassi offers a free trial and several paid options. All plans include full browser automation capabilities.

## Plans

| | Free Trial | BYOK | Starter | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $0 | $10/mo | $25/mo | $100/mo | $300/mo |
| **Managed credits** | Limited | — | Included | Included | Included |
| **Models** | Gemini 3 Pro | OpenAI models | All models | All models | All models |
| **Custom skills** | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| **Priority support** | — | — | — | yes | yes |

### Free Trial

Try dassi at no cost. Includes access to Gemini 3 Pro with limited managed credits. Good for trying dassi before committing to a paid plan.

### BYOK — $10/month or $12/year

Bring your own AI provider key. You pay the provider directly for model usage, and dassi charges a platform fee. Available billing intervals:

- **Monthly** — $10/month
- **Annual** — $12/year (save 90% vs monthly)

Supported providers include OpenAI (GPT-5, GPT-5 Mini, GPT-5 Nano), Anthropic, Google, and others you connect in **Settings > Connection**.

See [AI Models & Providers](/guides/connect-ai-provider/) for setup instructions.

### Starter — $25/month

Unlocks all managed models (Claude Sonnet 4.5, Claude Opus 4.6, Gemini 3 Flash, DeepSeek V3.2, GLM 4.7) with managed credits. No API key needed.

### Pro — $100/month

Higher credit allocation for heavy use. Includes priority support.

### Max — $300/month

The highest credit allocation for power users and teams with intensive automation needs.

## Managed credits

Managed credits cover the cost of AI model usage. Each message you send uses credits based on:

- The model you've selected (premium models cost more per message)
- The complexity of the task (longer conversations use more credits)
- Screenshot and page content processing

Credits refresh monthly with your billing cycle. You can track your usage in **Settings > Billing**.

## Running BYOK and a managed plan together

You can subscribe to both a BYOK plan and a managed plan at the same time. This is useful if you want to use your own API keys for some tasks and managed credits for others, or if you're switching between providers.

The **Current Plan** card in **Settings > Plan** shows both active subscriptions. Each has its own **Manage** button that opens the billing portal scoped to that subscription, so cancelling or updating one doesn't affect the other.

## Managing your subscription

1. Open the dassi sidebar.
2. Click **Settings** (gear icon).
3. Go to **Plan**.

The Plan tab has two sections — **Managed Plans** and **BYOK** — so you can browse and subscribe to each independently. The **Current Plan** card at the top always shows what's active.

To update payment details or cancel a subscription, click the **Manage** button next to the relevant plan in the Current Plan card.
