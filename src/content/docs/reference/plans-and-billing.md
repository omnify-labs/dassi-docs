dassi offers a free tier and several paid options. All plans include full browser automation capabilities.

## Plans

| | Free Beta | BYOK | Starter | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly** | $0 | $10/mo | $25/mo | $100/mo | $300/mo |
| **Yearly** | — | $60/yr | $250/yr | $1,000/yr | $3,000/yr |
| **Managed credits** | Limited | — | Included | Included | Included |
| **BYOK access** | — | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| **Priority support** | — | — | — | yes | yes |

### Free Beta

Try dassi at no cost with a limited credit allowance. Good for getting a feel for the extension before choosing a plan.

### BYOK — $10/month or $60/year

Bring your own API key from a supported provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others). You pay the model provider directly for usage; dassi charges $10/month for platform access. Paying annually costs $60/year — 50% off the monthly rate.

See [connecting an AI provider](/guides/connect-ai-provider/) for setup instructions.

### Starter — $25/month or $250/year

Unlocks managed AI models and credits so you don't need an API key. Includes BYOK access for the providers you prefer. Paying annually saves approximately 17%.

### Pro — $100/month or $1,000/year

Higher credit allocation for daily, intensive use. Includes BYOK access and priority support. Paying annually saves approximately 17%.

### Max — $300/month or $3,000/year

The highest credit allocation for power users and teams with heavy automation needs. Includes BYOK access and priority support. Paying annually saves approximately 17%.

## Annual billing

Choosing an annual plan costs less than paying month to month. The Plan tab has a **Monthly** / **Yearly** toggle that switches all four plan cards to show the annual price, with a "Save X%" badge and the struck-through monthly equivalent so you can compare easily.

## Managed credits

Managed credits cover AI model usage on Starter, Pro, and Max plans. Each message uses credits based on:

- The model selected (premium models cost more per message)
- The complexity of the task (longer conversations use more credits)
- Screenshot and page content processing

Credits refresh monthly with your billing cycle. You can track your usage in **Settings > Plan**.

## Managing your subscription

1. Open the dassi extension
2. Click **Settings** (gear icon)
3. Go to the **Plan** tab

From here you can:

- Use the **Monthly** / **Yearly** toggle to compare prices at both billing intervals.
- Click any plan card to select it, then click **Upgrade** to start a checkout.
- If you already have a subscription, clicking any card opens the Stripe billing portal where you can switch plans, update payment details, or cancel.
