The task dashboard at [app.dassi.ai](https://app.dassi.ai) is a web page that shows every task dassi is running, waiting on, or scheduled to run — across your whole browser, not just the tab you're looking at.

## Why use it

If you've asked dassi to do a few things at once — a task running quietly in a background tab, a plan waiting for your approval, a scheduled task due tomorrow morning — the dashboard puts all of it in one list instead of you hunting through tab groups to find what needs attention.

## Opening the dashboard

Visit [app.dassi.ai](https://app.dassi.ai) in any tab. It connects to the dassi extension running in the same browser automatically — there's nothing to sign in to or configure separately.

If dassi isn't installed, or is installed but disabled, the dashboard shows install steps and an **Add dassi to Chrome** button instead of your task list. After installing, click **I've installed it — reload**.

## What you'll see

Tasks are grouped into four buckets in the sidebar:

- **Needs you** — a plan is waiting for your approval, or dassi hit something it can't resolve on its own
- **Running** — dassi is actively working right now
- **Your Workflows** — [scheduled tasks](/guides/scheduled-tasks/) and their status: waiting for their next run, paused, awaiting your consent, needing attention, or that their last run failed
- **Done** — recently finished tasks

Click a bucket to filter the list, or select **All tasks** to see everything together.

Click any task to open its details in the right-hand rail. If the task has a live tab, click **Open in Chrome** to focus that tab and open its side panel — useful for approving a plan or picking up where dassi left off.

## Starting a new task

Type what you want done into the bar at the bottom of the dashboard and press **Enter**. dassi opens a new tab with the side panel already running your prompt — the same as typing directly into the sidebar, just without needing a tab open first.

## Tips

- The dashboard refreshes every few seconds while its tab is visible, so new tasks and status changes show up on their own.
- It only shows tasks from the browser it's running in — it can't see tasks from a different computer or Chrome profile.
